Questions

What is a Data Book?

A Data Book, also called a Manufacturing Data Record (MDR) or final documentation dossier, is the set of documents a supplier hands over with equipment: certificates, material certificates, welding and NDT records, test reports, drawings. The client sets the index and the numbering, and checks that every document is there. Read more.

Do my documents go to a server?

No. kornall runs on your Windows PC and works without an internet connection. Your documents, names and folders stay on your computer, which matters when they are under a client confidentiality agreement.

Is the Data Book module available today?

kornall 2.0 is available today with the map, numbering, duplicate detection and state report. The Data Book module (templates, index import, completeness check, PDF compilation, revisions) is in development and released to early-access users first. It is included in Professional and Team at no extra cost.

I am not in industry. Is kornall for me?

Yes. The Personal plan is made for anyone who wants clean, numbered folders without duplicates: students, freelancers, researchers, photographers. See kornall for everyone.

What happens after the 7-day trial?

Choose a plan to keep using kornall. Nothing is charged automatically, and your folders stay exactly as they are.

Can kornall delete my files by mistake?

No. Every change is shown for review first, removed files go to the Recycle Bin, and moves, renames and copies can be undone.
