Pricing questions

How does the trial work?

Install kornall and use every feature for 7 days. No card, no automatic payment. When the trial ends, choose a plan to continue.

How do I cancel?

From your account, in one click. Monthly plans stop at the end of the paid month, yearly plans at the end of the paid year. No further payment is taken.

Can I switch from monthly to yearly?

Yes, from your account. The yearly price applies from your next renewal.

Do prices include VAT?

Personal prices include VAT. Business prices exclude VAT; EU companies outside France with a valid VAT number are invoiced without VAT (reverse charge).
