Pricing questions

How does the trial work?

Install kornall and use every feature for 7 days. No card, no automatic payment. When the trial ends, choose a plan to continue.

Why does Professional cost more than Personal?

Professional adds the Data Book module: templates, index import, completeness check, PDF compilation and revisions. It is priced for companies that deliver documentation to their clients.

I buy Professional today. Do I get the Data Book module?

Yes. It is delivered as a free update to Professional and Team subscribers. Until then, you use the full kornall workspace.

How does Team billing work?

€15 per user per month, billed yearly, from 5 to 50 users. Add users anytime; they are billed pro rata until your renewal date.

How do I cancel?

From your account, in one click. The plan stays active until the end of the period already paid. No further payment is taken.

Do prices include VAT?

Personal prices include VAT. Professional, Team and Enterprise prices exclude VAT. EU companies outside France with a valid VAT number are invoiced without VAT (reverse charge), and customers outside the EU pay no EU VAT.
