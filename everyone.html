Questions

Do I need the Data Book features?

No. Personal includes the whole kornall workspace: map, numbering, duplicates and the state report. Data Book features are for industrial documentation and come with Professional.

What does "up to 2 PCs" mean?

One Personal licence activates kornall on two computers you use, for example a desktop and a laptop. You can move an activation from your account.

Can I cancel?

Yes, at any time from your account. kornall keeps working until the end of the period you paid for.

Which systems are supported?

Windows 10 and Windows 11, 64-bit.
